Flydubai Flight FZ1073 Diverts Following In-Flight Engine System Anomaly & Emergency Protocol

On September 30, 2026, Flydubai Flight FZ1073, operated by a Boeing 737 MAX 8 aircraft originating from Dubai International Airport (DXB), executed a safe priority diversion to Muscat International Airport (MCT) following flight deck advisory warnings regarding engine oil pressure telemetry. All 168 passengers and 6 crew members disembarked safely without injuries.

Executive Incident Synopsis
  • Precautionary Shut Down: Flight crew declared 'PAN-PAN' at FL310 after power plant parameter alerts.
  • Safe Diversion: Safe touchdown executed on Runway 26R at MCT with emergency services on standby.
  • Investigation Status: UAE General Civil Aviation Authority (GCAA) and Omani CAA have initiated a joint preliminary inquiry.

Investigation & Authority Statements

Official press releases, regulator advisories, and preliminary technical reports.

Official Statement #GCAA-2026-0930 Sept 30, 2026 - 11:30 UTC

Preliminary Incident Notification by GCAA Air Accident Investigation Sector

"The General Civil Aviation Authority (GCAA) confirms that Flydubai Flight FZ1073, a Boeing 737 MAX 8 bound from Dubai to its destination, safely diverted to Muscat International Airport on September 30, 2026 following an engine indication alert. Preliminary findings confirm no hull damage or crew/passenger injury. GCAA investigators are coordinating with the Sultanate of Oman Civil Aviation Authority to extract Flight Data Recorder (FDR) telemetry."

Flydubai Media Release Sept 30, 2026 - 10:15 UTC

Precautionary Diversion of Flight FZ1073 to Muscat

"Flydubai confirms that Flight FZ1073 on 30 September 2026 diverted to Muscat International Airport (MCT) as a precautionary measure due to an engine indication on the flight deck. The aircraft landed safely and passengers disembarked via standard gate protocols. A replacement aircraft was dispatched to Muscat to accommodate passengers for their onward travel."

OEM Technical Advisory Support Oct 01, 2026 - 08:00 UTC

CFM International & Boeing Technical Field Support

"Boeing and CFM International technical support teams are in communication with the operator and air safety regulators regarding the telemetry logs from Flight FZ1073. Engineering advisory protocols remain standard while component inspection proceeds."
